# Proofly Product Vision

## One-sentence definition

**Proofly is a global proof-of-work network for junior and early-career software developers and startups: talent earns trusted opportunities through independently reviewed real work, while companies discover and hire through evidence rather than self-reported resumes alone.**

## Product purpose

Proofly is the trusted work identity layer for people whose ability should be judged by what they can actually deliver. It turns real project work into structured, reviewable, portable evidence and helps startups move from relevant evidence to a bounded paid opportunity. Proofly improves the signal available before human hiring judgment; it does not replace that judgment.

The long-term direction is global, while initial production availability remains limited to an explicit approved market register. Market, language, payment, tax, worker-classification, privacy, support, moderation, and provider readiness must be established before a market is enabled.

## First product wedge

Proofly begins with **junior and early-career software developers** and **startups or small product teams**. The initial work categories are frontend, full-stack, backend, and product engineering projects. English and USD are first, with internationalization designed into the model rather than assumed to mean every market is supported.

This wedge is intentionally narrower than a broad professional network. Expansion to designers, analysts, marketers, or other knowledge workers is deferred until each category has a credible evidence model and qualified reviewers.

## Audiences and role clarity

| Audience | What the role does | Why the role matters |
|---|---|---|
| Talent | Builds and submits real software work, receives review, controls public proof, and pursues paid opportunities | Talent can show specific ability with evidence rather than relying only on pedigree or self-reported claims. |
| Company | Defines realistic work, evaluates relevant evidence, and invites a bounded paid trial before a larger engagement | Companies can reduce early hiring uncertainty without treating a profile, activity count, or opaque score as a final decision. |
| Reviewer | Applies expertise to a submission using a transparent rubric, conflict controls, and actionable feedback | Reviewers make evidence more credible and help talent understand how to improve; their accountability is part of the trust model. |

A person may participate in more than one capacity, but the action being taken and the authorization required must remain explicit. Organization confirmation, reviewer approval, payout eligibility, identity assurance, and work verification are separate signals. Proofly does not combine them into a hidden universal trust or talent score.

## Core problem

Early-career software developers may be able to deliver useful work but have limited ways to make that ability legible before they accumulate conventional experience. Resumes are self-reported, portfolios vary in depth and context, certificates can show completion without delivery, and activity or contribution signals do not by themselves establish authorship, quality, or fit. Startups and small product teams therefore face an uneven evidence problem when evaluating early-career talent. Reviewers need a consistent, accountable way to turn expert judgment into useful evidence without becoming an opaque gatekeeper.

Proofly addresses the gap by connecting a specific piece of real work to its submission context, a qualified human review, an explainable proof record, and a potential paid outcome. This is a product hypothesis that requires research validation; it is not a claim that existing hiring workflows have already been proven to change.

## Product promise

> **Build a trusted work identity from real projects, and move from independently reviewed evidence to a relevant paid opportunity.**

Proofly helps talent understand what to build next, prove specific skills, receive useful feedback, and reach paid opportunities. It helps companies define a realistic work signal, find relevant verified talent, compare evidence fairly, and begin with a bounded paid trial. It helps reviewers provide high-quality, accountable feedback and build a trusted reviewer reputation.

## Proof-to-opportunity loop

The loop has a clear start and finish. It begins with a relevant, bounded real-world challenge and finishes when an evidence-supported paid opportunity creates a new auditable outcome.

1. Talent chooses a relevant challenge or project.
2. Talent submits a working solution and supporting evidence.
3. An approved reviewer evaluates the submission against a transparent rubric, checks for conflicts, and gives actionable feedback.
4. Proofly records the eligible evidence in the talent's Proof Graph, linking the exact submission, review decision, skill, reviewer, and outcome. Public proof requires a valid review chain and an appropriate visibility decision.
5. A startup discovers relevant talent through explainable evidence rather than claims alone.
6. The company and talent may begin a bounded, compensated paid trial with clear scope and acceptance criteria.
7. A completed trial may progress to a contract, and the resulting outcome becomes an auditable reputation event that can strengthen future opportunities.

The loop is human-accountable. AI may assist with approved summarization, tagging, matching suggestions, or safety detection when its sources, uncertainty, model information, and correction path are visible. AI cannot independently verify proof, decide hiring or rejection, approve reviewers, move money, enforce an account action, or resolve a dispute.

## Differentiation

Proofly is not a resume site with another profile page, not a generic job board, and not a generic freelance marketplace. It is an evidence-centered network whose differentiating chain is **real work → qualified human review → explainable proof → bounded paid opportunity**.

It respects the context provided by professional networks, code hosts, portfolios, learning platforms, assessments, and existing marketplaces without treating their activity, presentation, completion, or popularity signals as automatic proof. Proof provenance, reviewer accountability, conflict controls, privacy-preserving visibility, and the transition from proof to paid work are central to the product direction.

## Non-goals for the first release

Proofly will not launch as a general-purpose social network, generic job board, open-ended proposal marketplace, course library, or platform for every profession. It will not use a single opaque score to decide who is trustworthy or employable. It will not automate final proof verification, hiring, rejection, reviewer approval, payment, enforcement, disputes, or account actions. It will not treat unpaid production work or pay-to-apply mechanics as an acceptable growth path. It will not imply that every global market is enabled before the market register and required readiness reviews are complete.

## Product principles

Proofly follows these principles:

- **Evidence before claims.** Work context, provenance, and review evidence come before self-description and activity counts.
- **Human judgment remains accountable.** Authorized people own verification, hiring, reviewer decisions, and consequential actions.
- **Explain important signals.** Recommendations and reputation signals must show their evidence, context, uncertainty, and limits.
- **Keep signals separate.** Identity assurance, work verification, reviewer approval, payment eligibility, outcomes, and internal risk signals have different purposes and visibility.
- **Respect user control and privacy.** People preview what becomes public, private evidence stays restricted, and collection is limited to the current outcome.
- **Make paid work fair and bounded.** Paid trials have scope, compensation, and acceptance criteria; Proofly does not rely on unpaid production work or pay-to-apply mechanics.
- **Make the next action obvious.** Talent, companies, and reviewers should understand what to do next and why it matters.
- **Build trust through consistent details.** Rubrics, conflicts, provenance, corrections, and status changes are explicit and auditable.
- **Design for access and global readiness.** Keyboard use, mobile workflows, assistive technology, localization, and market readiness are foundational constraints.

## Success direction

The first release is successful when the core loop shows credible progress for all three roles, without requiring unsupported claims. Directional measures include: eligible first-time talent completing a bounded proof and receiving a qualified review; reviewed submissions producing complete rubric-based evidence and actionable feedback; target companies reaching relevant verified candidates; bounded paid trials beginning after evidence-led discovery; talent and companies returning for a repeat opportunity; and trust signals such as disputes, fraud, fake reviews, privacy incidents, unpaid-work reports, and payment-integrity failures remaining within approved thresholds.

The thresholds, cohorts, owners, market scope, and evidence artifacts are launch decisions to be approved and recorded. Until that evidence exists, the product direction remains a set of explicit hypotheses.

## Product boundaries

Proofly's boundary is the evidence chain and the accountability around it. The first release covers bounded software work, structured submissions, qualified human review, proof records, evidence-led discovery, applications, paid trials, and the auditable transition to contract where approved market and payment capabilities allow it.

The first release excludes unsupported markets, unapproved payment or payout flows, public exposure of private evidence, hidden risk or trust scoring, automatic inference of ability or identity, and any client-side or AI-generated statement treated as verification truth. Public proof requires a valid submission and completed human review with the appropriate consent and visibility decision. Payment truth comes from verified provider state. Reputation changes reference auditable events, and corrections do not rewrite history.

## Research status

The concept and workflow are hypotheses until real participants validate them. Phase 01 does not claim market validation, implementation completion, legal approval, or production readiness. Research must test whether junior developers complete bounded proof work, companies understand and trust reviewed evidence, reviewers find the work and incentive acceptable, paid trials reduce uncertainty without creating unpaid labor, and users can explain why a proof record is credible.
