# Proofly

## Product definition

**Proofly is a global proof-of-work network for junior and early-career software developers and startups: talent earns trusted opportunities through independently reviewed real work, while companies discover and hire through evidence rather than self-reported resumes alone.**

## Product purpose

Proofly helps software talent build a trusted work identity from real projects and helps startups evaluate relevant evidence before human hiring judgment. The product connects real work to qualified human review, explainable proof, and a bounded paid opportunity. Proofly supports human judgment; it does not replace it.

## First product wedge

The first release is limited to junior and early-career software developers and startups or small product teams. Initial work covers frontend, full-stack, backend, and product engineering projects. English and USD are first, with internationalization designed into the model. The long-term direction is global, but each production market requires an approved readiness register before it is enabled.

## Roles and outcomes

| Audience | Role | Primary outcome |
|---|---|---|
| Talent | Submits real software work, receives review, controls public proof, and pursues paid opportunities | Prove specific skills with credible evidence and reach trusted opportunities without relying only on pedigree |
| Company | Defines realistic work, evaluates evidence, and may invite a bounded paid trial | Reduce early hiring uncertainty by comparing relevant evidence fairly |
| Reviewer | Evaluates submissions against a transparent rubric and gives actionable feedback | Make evidence more credible while building an accountable reviewer reputation |

## Proof-to-opportunity loop

1. Talent chooses a relevant bounded challenge.
2. Talent submits a working solution and supporting evidence.
3. A qualified human reviewer evaluates the work against a transparent rubric and provides feedback.
4. Proofly records eligible evidence in the talent's Proof Graph with provenance and the appropriate visibility decision.
5. A startup discovers relevant talent through evidence rather than claims alone.
6. The company and talent may begin a scoped, compensated paid trial.
7. A completed trial may progress to a contract and create a new auditable outcome.

AI remains an assistive layer for approved, transparent, source-linked, and correctable tasks such as summarization, tagging, matching suggestions, or safety detection. AI does not independently verify proof, decide hiring or rejection, approve reviewers, move money, enforce account actions, or resolve disputes.

## Product boundaries

Proofly is not a resume site, generic job board, generic freelance marketplace, general-purpose social network, course library, or universal talent-scoring system. It does not launch every profession at once, rely on unpaid production work or pay-to-apply mechanics, expose private evidence publicly, or automate final verification, hiring, payment, enforcement, disputes, or account actions.

Public proof requires a valid submission and completed human review with the appropriate consent and visibility decision. Identity assurance, organization confirmation, reviewer approval, payout eligibility, and work verification are separate signals. Payment truth comes from verified provider state, and reputation changes reference auditable events. “Global” describes the intended network direction, not a claim that every market is launched or supported.

## Build rule

Proofly is built in small, verifiable phases. Each phase must preserve completed work, remain within its approved scope, include relevant acceptance evidence, and stop after its completion commit. Phase 01 is documentation-only; it does not claim implemented behavior, validated market demand, legal approval, or production readiness.
